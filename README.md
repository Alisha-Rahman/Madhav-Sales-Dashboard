# 📊 Madhav Ecommerce Sales Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The Madhav Ecommerce Sales Dashboard is an interactive Power BI dashboard created to analyze ecommerce sales performance, profit, quantity, customers, payment methods, categories, and geographical performance.

🚀 This is my first Power BI dashboard, created as part of my Power BI practice and learning journey.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies used

- 📊 Power BI Desktop
- 🔄 Power Query
- 🧮 DAX
- 🧹 Data Cleaning & Transformation
- 🔗 Data Modeling
- 📈 Data Visualization
- 🎛️ Interactive Filters & Slicers

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze sales and profit performance
- Identify sales performance across different states
- Analyze payment methods
- Understand category and sub-category performance
- Analyze customer-wise sales
- Track important business KPIs
- Create an interactive dashboard for business analysis

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 🔴 Business Problem

Ecommerce businesses generate large amounts of sales data across different states, customers, categories, sub-categories, and payment methods. Analyzing this data manually can make it difficult to understand sales performance, profitability, and areas that need attention.

### 🎯 Goal of the Dashboard

The goal of this dashboard is to transform raw ecommerce data into an interactive and easy-to-understand visual report that helps analyze sales, quantity, profit, customers, payment methods, and geographical performance.

### 📌 Dashboard Description

#### 💰 Key Performance Indicators

- Sum of Amount
- Sum of Quantity
- Sum of Profit
- Sum of Average Order Value (AOV)

#### 🗺️ Sales by State

Shows the sales amount across different states and helps compare geographical sales performance.

#### 💳 Quantity by Payment Mode

Shows the distribution of quantity across different payment methods such as COD, UPI, Debit Card, Credit Card, and EMI.

#### 📅 Profit by Month

Shows monthly profit performance and helps identify changes in profitability throughout the year.

#### 👥 Sales by Customer

Shows the sales amount associated with different customers.

#### 📦 Quantity by Category

Shows the distribution of sales quantity across different categories such as Clothing, Electronics, and Furniture.

#### 🏷️ Profit by Sub-Category

Compares profit across different sub-categories such as Printers, Bookcases, Saree, Accessories, and Tables.

### 🎛️ Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes interactive filters for:

- Quarter
- State

### 💡 Business Impact & Insights

The dashboard makes it easier to identify:

- 📈 Sales performance across different states
- 💰 Monthly profit trends
- 🛍️ Categories and sub-categories contributing to sales and profit
- 👥 Customers generating higher sales
- 💳 Popular payment methods
- 🔎 Areas where further business analysis may be useful

These insights can support data-driven analysis and help businesses better understand their sales and profitability patterns.
